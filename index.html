Version 2.5.2 rules
Move colored pieces through connected empty spaces. Reach a matching gate on any edge to clear a piece. Not every row or column has a gate. Each level has a move limit; Undo restores the move you rewound. Press and drag a piece to see it follow your finger, or use tap controls. Holding is temporary parking: a held piece must return to the exact space it came from before it can move or escape. Brown crates take two hits to break. A locked blue piece shows how many other blue pieces must clear before it unlocks; then it becomes a normal blue piece. Linked pieces show one link between them, move together, and can clear through a reachable blue gate in one move.
