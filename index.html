Prototype rules
Tap a colored piece that is exposed from above. If its color matches the exit at the top of that column, it clears immediately. Otherwise it goes into one of four holding slots. Three matching held colors clear automatically. Chained pieces must be cleared as a pair. Crates take two taps. Locks open after the matching key color has been cleared.
