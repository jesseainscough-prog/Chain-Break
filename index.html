Version 2 rules
Move colored pieces through connected empty spaces. Reach the matching gate on the top row to clear a piece. You can also tap the matching gate after selecting a piece. Holding is temporary: move a piece into a slot, then bring it back to any empty board space later. Crates are fixed barriers. The blue lock opens after two blue pieces clear. Brown crates take two hits to break. The two chained blue pieces move together as one unit and clear together through the blue gate.
