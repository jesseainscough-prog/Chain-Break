Version 2.4.2 rules
Move colored pieces through connected empty spaces. Reach the matching gate on the top row to clear a piece. Press and drag a piece to see it follow your finger, or use tap controls. Holding is temporary: move a piece into a slot, then bring it back to any empty board space later. Brown crates take two hits to break. A locked blue piece shows how many other blue pieces must clear before it unlocks; then it becomes a normal blue piece. Linked pieces show one link between them, move together, and can clear through a reachable blue gate in one move.

How to play

Drag a colored piece to the matching gate.

Try it on the board above — the lesson disappears after your first successful drag.
