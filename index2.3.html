Version 2.3 rules
Move colored pieces through connected empty spaces. Reach the matching gate on the top row to clear a piece. You can also press and drag a piece directly to its matching gate when a route is open. Holding is temporary: move a piece into a slot, then bring it back to any empty board space later. Brown crates take two hits to break. A locked blue piece shows how many other blue pieces must clear before it unlocks; then it becomes a normal blue piece. Linked pieces show one link between them, move together, and can clear through a reachable blue gate in one move.
